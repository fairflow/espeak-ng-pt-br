# French Phrasebook - Greetings
# Practical phrases for real-world situations
# Difficulty levels: A:9
# Format: french | english | [ipa]

Bonjour | Hello | [bɔ̃ʒˈuʁ]
Bonsoir | Good evening | [bɔ̃swˈaʁ]
Salut | Hi | [salˈy]
Comment allez-vous? | How are you? | [kɔmˌɑ̃ alˈevˈu]
Comment ça va? | How are you? | [kɔmˌɑ̃ sa vˈa]
Ça va | I'm fine | [sa vˈa]
Ça va bien | I'm doing well | [sa vˈa bjˈɛ̃]
Je vais bien, merci | I'm fine, thank you | [ʒə- vˈɛ bjˈɛ̃  mɛʁsˈi]
Et vous? | And you? | [e vˈu]
